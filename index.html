Executive Summary

Three trends have reshaped the high-frequency store landscape in the past two years: increased multi-sourcing within wholesale, the expansion of hard discounters introducing new competitive pressure on price, and consumers trading up to next sizes and forms.

To win in this channel, brands must win with Sari-Sari Store Owners through three big bets:

Drive category assortment depth across store tiers

02

03

01
Jobs-to-Be-Done #1 · Assortment

Help me stay ahead of other Sari-Sari stores
and the Discounter with the right variety of SKUs.

Assortment is her competitive moat. The Discounter wins on price but has gaps on trusted brands — making variety the SSS owner's sharpest edge against both neighboring stores and hard discounters.

Core Insight

The Discounter's threat and her edge

Hard discounters are accelerating expansion into residential barangays. Their strength is price via housebrands; their gap is branded assortment. The SSS owner wins when she is kumpleto — complete. Out-of-stock on a core SKU doesn't just lose the sale; it loses the customer.

Implication Drive more categories and variants into the channel so SSS Owners stay ahead of local competition.

Recommendations

Priority: Low-penetration categories in Wholesale Drive assortment starting with the categories showing the steepest carrying rate decline at wholesale tier.
Proximity-based SKU push Near wet markets → food-adjacent and cooking-related categories. Near service establishments → categories relevant to their customer's activity. Near schools → hygiene and grooming categories.
Seasonality sell-in Rainy season → hygiene-benefit variants. Year-end season → categories with high gifting and celebration occasions. Summer → refreshing and grooming categories.
Map high-competition clusters Identify barangays with multiple SSS + nearby Discounters. Sell in missing core categories, then introduce next variants in existing ones.
New SKU demand generation Bundle new SKUs with proven fast-movers. Run retailer trial programs with proof-of-performance data to disrupt autopilot replenishment behavior.
02
Jobs-to-Be-Done #2 · Financial Ease

Make it easy for me to build stock,
strengthen margins, and keep my cash moving weekly.

Weekly cash velocity is the heartbeat of her business. Bundle packs are her preferred mechanism — not just for margin, but as reinvestable capital that keeps her shelves stocked between trips.

Core Insight

Bundle packs as margin engine + cash flow tool

Multi-unit bundle packs (e.g. 6+1, 11+1 configs) are the most sought-after promo format — preferred over percentage discounts or peso-savings messaging. To SSS owners, bundles are not a discount; they are a freebie to sell at full price, generating additional capital that flows back into next-trip restocking.

Broad-based bundle pack availability in wholesale stores shows strong positive correlation with store penetration growth across the portfolio.

Implication To grow store penetration, drive bundle pack availability across wholesale. Match her replenishment cycle: weekly.

Recommendations

Targeted bundle push in wholesale Focus on SKUs with simultaneous share and store penetration decline for a concentrated bundle pack push in wholesale channels.

03
Jobs-to-Be-Done #3 · Business Continuity

Keep my store open and ensure
I don't go out-of-stock.

Operational certainty governs supplier choice. She multi-sources across 2–3 channels monthly to manage out-of-stock risk — and once a supplier earns reliability, promo chassis and rebates determine share of wallet.

What drives supplier choice — in order
#1
Service
Next-day delivery and nearby stores minimize transportation costs and store closures during restocking trips.
#2
Assortment
One-stop shop for all core SKUs — minimizes out-of-stock risk across her full category set in a single trip.
#3
Promo Availability
Bundle packs are freebies she sells at full price — they are capital, not just promotions.
#4
Chassis
Cashbacks, rebates, and sale events increase stock and capital for the next trip. Switching happens once reliability is proven.
Implication Strengthen partnerships with the wholesale and retail channels that matter most to her restocking behavior — service and chassis are the levers.

How she shops across channels

Within a month, she shops 2–3 different supplier channels based on who best delivers on service and assortment:

Discounter — rising frequency
Walking distance, easy navigation, under 5 mins in-store. Proximity is the primary driver.
Wholesale A — preferred for ordering
Next-day delivery, site/app ordering — she doesn't have to close her store. Rebate chassis drives loyalty.
Wholesale B — secondary, for bulk events
Least frequented for walk-in; requires store closure and commute. Visited primarily during sale events.
Consumer Voice

As much as possible, I buy what I can from the Discounter. It's just a short walk away from my store and it's easy to navigate. It takes less than five minutes to finish buying.

SSS Owner · Discounter primary shopper

It's so easy to order from my wholesale supplier. The site shows me everything that I need for my store. I can get everything the next day as long as I order in the morning.

SSS Owner · Wholesale ordering

I want my store to remain open as much as possible. I could lose more sales if I go to the supermarket more frequently — I'd have to close my shop to commute there.

SSS Owner · Store closure aversion

I enjoy the rebate perks because I can save enough points to buy more for my store during Christmas. I hold the points until year-end because that's when I need to buy more stock.

SSS Owner · Loyalty chassis

I make a scheduled trip whenever there's a big sale — there are huge discounts on items I need for my store, so I make sure to buy a little more than I usually would.

SSS Owner · Sale event shopper

I only buy from my wholesale supplier what I know will sell quickly. I worry about slow-moving inventory if I buy all my variants from one place.

SSS Owner · Basket concentration risk

Recommendations

Surface promotion support at the channel level Highlight bundle and promo availability behind the SKUs with the steepest store penetration decline in wholesale partner plans.
Activate rebate and points chassis Rebates are perceived as capital returns for the next trip — they compound loyalty when tied to high-stocking windows like year-end.
Leverage sale event loading Periodic bulk sale events are planned trips — an opportunity to drive deep loading of bundle packs and next sizes.
Strengthen digital ordering capability App and site-based ordering is gaining share especially in larger stores. Friction-free ordering with next-day delivery is table stakes for retaining her wallet.
